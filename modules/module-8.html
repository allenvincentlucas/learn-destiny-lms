Module 8: Destiny Discover, Collections & Digital Integration

The student-facing side of Destiny: Collections, the eBook reader, Canvas/Google Classroom embedding, and Parent Portal.

PrerequisitesModules 1–7
Est. Time1.5 hours
FormatSelf-paced reading + knowledge check
Share this module Link copied — paste it into your Instagram Story or post.

Welcome to Module 8: Destiny Discover, Collections & Digital Integration of the Follett Destiny Library Management System (LMS) training program. This module covers student-facing discovery interfaces, digital curation with Collections by Destiny, eBook/audiobook integration, and personalized reading engagement tools in Destiny Engage (Follett Software, "Destiny Discover").

Providing an intuitive student portal fosters reading engagement, promotes digital literacy, and enables educators to share curated learning resources seamlessly across classrooms (Follett Community, "Welcome to the New Destiny Discover").

What You'll Learn

  • Customize the Destiny Discover homepage ribbon layout and apply a student-facing theme.
  • Build a thematic Collection combining catalog titles, web links, and video resources for a classroom unit.
  • Explain the accessibility features (text-to-speech, dyslexia-friendly fonts, WCAG compliance) of the integrated eBook reader.
  • Configure LTI integration so Destiny Discover content can be embedded in Canvas or Google Classroom.
  • Set up parent-portal access using synchronized guardian contact fields from the SIS.

2. Student Portal, Digital Curation & eBooks

A. Destiny Discover Portal Configuration

  • Unified Single-Search Interface: Provides students and teachers with a single search bar to discover physical books, Follett eBooks, audiobooks, open-access web resources, and subscription databases (Follett Software, "Destiny Discover").
  • Homepage Ribbon Layout Customization (Catalog > Search Setup > Digital Setup): Customizing homepage ribbons (e.g., Recently Added, Popular Titles, EBooks, Browse by Topic, Featured Collections) and applying student themes (Follett School Solutions, "Configure Destiny Discover").

B. Collections by Destiny

  • Resource Curation Boards: Creating thematic digital collection boards containing web links, videos, documents, and catalog titles for classroom research projects (Follett Software, "Collections by Destiny").
  • Sharing & Privacy Controls: Setting collection visibility (Public, School, Private) and collaborating with teachers to embed collections into curriculum units (Follett Software, "Collections by Destiny").

C. eBook Reader & Destiny Engage

  • Integrated eBook Reader: Follett eBooks open automatically within the browser or Destiny Discover app without requiring separate software downloads (Follett School Solutions, "New to Destiny").
  • Destiny Engage Reading Tools: Destiny 24.0 adds a Free Reading club type that lets students track reading hours against any book, plus new Book Club metrics (minutes, pages, streaks) to drive engagement (Follett Community, "Destiny 24.0 Readiness Kit").

D. Discover WCAG Accessibility, LMS Integration & Parent Portal

  • Destiny Discover Accessibility & WCAG Compliance:
    • WCAG 2.1 Level AA Compliance: Destiny Discover conforms to WCAG 2.1 AA and Section 508 guidelines, offering full keyboard navigation, screen-reader status announcements (JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver), and scalable typography (Follett Software, "Destiny 24.0: Product Updates").
    • Accessible Digital Reader: The integrated Follett eBook reader features built-in text-to-speech audio, adjustable font sizes, high-contrast display modes, and dyslexia-friendly font options (Follett School Solutions, "New to Destiny").
  • LMS Integration: Google Classroom & Canvas LTI:
    • Canvas / Schoology LTI Integration (Setup > Sites > SSO Configuration > LTI): Registers Destiny as an LTI tool, allowing students and teachers to search and embed library titles directly within Canvas courses (Follett School Solutions, "Single Sign-On").
    • Google Classroom & Thin Common Cartridge Sharing: Teachers share catalog items or entire Collections directly to Google Classroom via one-click share buttons, or export Thin Common Cartridge files into LMS course modules (Follett Software, "Sharing Collections").
  • Destiny Parent Portal Setup:
    • Parent Roster Linkage: Primary parent/guardian email addresses registered in Skyward or PowerSchool map directly to student records in Destiny (Email 1 and Email 2) (Finalsite).
    • Parent Portal Access: Parents log in at DestinyDiscover.com using their registered email credentials to view their child's active loans, due dates, hold queues, and unpaid fines (Thrillshare).

4. Practical Hands-On Learning Activities

  • Activity 8.1 - Customizing Destiny Discover Homepage Ribbons: Navigate to Catalog > Search Setup > Digital > Digital Setup > Maintenance > Setup, reorder homepage ribbons, enable "Popular Titles" and "Featured Collections", and preview the changes in student view (Follett School Solutions, "Configure Destiny Discover").
  • Activity 8.2 - Building a Thematic Destiny Collection: Open Collections by Destiny, create a new public collection titled "Ecology & Climate Research", add 3 library books, 2 web article links, and 1 YouTube video link, and attach academic standards (Follett Software, "Collections by Destiny").

  • A. Admin > Access Levels
  • B. Catalog > Search Setup > Digital Setup

Q2. Which feature lets a teacher build a curated board of catalog titles, web links, and videos for a research unit?

  • A. Destiny Engage
  • B. Collections by Destiny
  • C. WebPath Express
  • D. Resource Value Report
Show answer
Correct answer: B

Q3. What accessibility feature is built directly into the Follett eBook reader?

  • A. Barcode scanning
  • B. Text-to-speech, adjustable font size, and dyslexia-friendly fonts
  • C. Automated overdue notices
  • D. LDAP role provisioning
Show answer
Correct answer: B

Q4. How does a Canvas or Schoology course get access to embedded Destiny library titles?

  • A. Through LTI integration configured under Setup > Sites > SSO Configuration
  • B. By manually emailing PDF catalogs to teachers
  • C. Through the Patron Import Converter
  • D. Through the Weeding Log report
Show answer
Correct answer: A

Q5. A parent asks how they can see their child's due dates and fines from home. Walk through where that data originates and how the parent accesses it.

Show key points
Primary/secondary guardian email fields, synced from the SIS (e.g., PowerSchool or Skyward) into Destiny's Email 1/Email 2 patron fields, power the Destiny Parent Portal; the parent logs in at the portal with their registered email to view active loans, due dates, hold queues, and fines.

Q6. A teacher wants students to see library resources without leaving Google Classroom. What two sharing options does Destiny support for this, and how do they differ?

Show key points
One-click sharing of catalog items/Collections directly to Google Classroom, or exporting a Thin Common Cartridge file for import into an LMS course module — the first is a direct live share, the second is a portable file-based import.
