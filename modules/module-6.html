Module 6: Inventory Management

Run a section inventory without closing the library, and connect the results into your weeding workflow.

PrerequisitesModules 1–5
Est. Time2 hours
FormatSelf-paced reading + knowledge check

Welcome to Module 6: Inventory Management of the Follett Destiny Library Management System (LMS) training program. This module details how to plan, initiate, conduct, and finalize library collection audits and district asset inventories in both Library Manager and Resource Manager (School Library Talk with Megan).

Regular inventory audits account for physical resources, identify missing or stolen items, update catalog copy statuses, and provide accurate collection valuation data for administration (Follett School Solutions, "Start a Library Inventory").

What You'll Learn

  • Initiate a scoped library inventory using call number, sublocation, or resource type filters.
  • Execute an inventory using live and/or offline (VersaScan/Follett Remote) barcode scanning.
  • Correctly process unaccounted-for items, including marking items Lost and linking results to weeding.
  • Sequence a year-end inventory using the four-phase checklist (Recall & Freeze, Database Prep, Physical Scanning, Reconciliation).
  • Interpret a Resource Value Report, including how straight-line depreciation is calculated.

2. Conducting Inventory Audits

A. Initiating an Inventory (Admin > Inventory or Back Office > Inventory)

  • Scope Selection: Define inventory limits by Call Number ranges (e.g., FIC A - FIC Z), Circulation Types, Sublocations (e.g., Graphic Novels), or Resource Types (LeRoy; Follett School Solutions, "Start a Library Inventory").
  • Exclusion Filters: Exclude items recently seen, checked in, or cataloged on or after a specified date to prevent duplicate counting (Follett School Solutions, "Start a Library Inventory").

B. Executing the Audit & Scanning Barcodes

  • Live Scanning: Scanning barcodes directly into the Destiny web interface using handheld scanners (Mrs H Media, "How To Do Inventory").
  • Offline Scanning: Collecting scans wirelessly using Follett Remote, VersaScan devices, or mobile barcode scanners, then uploading batch barcode files (Follett Community, "Conducting an Inventory").
  • Unbarcoded Items: Recording manual counts for unbarcoded or consumable resources in Resource Manager (Follett School Solutions, "Conduct an Inventory").

C. Handling Unaccounted-For Items & Finalizing

  • Accounted For vs. Unaccounted For: Destiny automatically moves scanned items to "Accounted For" status (Follett School Solutions, "Conduct an Inventory").
  • Finalizing Inventory: Reviewing unaccounted-for items, choosing whether to mark missing items as "Lost" or leave them unchanged, and generating official Job Summary audit reports (The Timer Pitt).

Module 6 Supplement: Weeding Integration, Annual Inventory Calendar & Financial Audit Valuation

A. Linking Inventory Results to Weeding Decisions

Inventory audits directly inform collection maintenance and weeding workflows:

  • Converting Missing Items to Weeded Status: When finalizing an inventory (Admin > Inventory > View In-Progress & Completed Inventories), items listed on the "Unaccounted For" page can be batch marked as "Lost" or "Deleted" (Follett School Solutions, "FAQs Library Manager Inventory"). Selecting the Track as weeded option transfers missing records directly to the Weeding Log, maintaining replacement valuation records for ordering (Follett Software, "Generate a Weeding Log").
  • CREW MUSTIE Evaluation During Physical Audits: Damaged, worn, or obsolete books identified during shelf scanning are pulled, added to a temporary Resource List, evaluated against CREW MUSTIE criteria (Misleading, Ugly, Superseded, Trivial, Irrelevant, Elsewhere), and batch deleted under Catalog > Update Copies > Batch Delete (Follett Software, "Simplify Your Library Management").

B. Annual Inventory Calendar & Year-End Planning Checklist

Establishing a structured inventory schedule prevents library closure backlogs and maintains data accuracy:

  • Best-Practice Scheduling Timeline:
    • Fall & Winter (Terms 1–2): Execute rolling, section-by-section inventories (e.g., Fiction A–G in October, Non-Fiction 500s in January, 1-to-1 Chromebook audits mid-term) to maintain continuous collection accuracy without closing the library (Follett Community, "Prepare for End of School Year").
    • Spring (May–June): Perform Year-End Full Collection Inventory and asset reconciliation (Follett Community, "Take Control of Year-End Asset Management").
  • Year-End Inventory Action Checklist:
    • 1. Phase 1 - Recall & Freeze (3 Weeks Out): Issue circulation recall notices for all student/staff loans, freeze interlibrary loans, and establish a firm due date (Follett Community, "Prepare for End of School Year").
    • 2. Phase 2 - Database Prep (1 Week Out): Run a full system backup, catalog all newly arrived materials, and clear temporary hold shelves (Follett Community, "Prepare for End of School Year").
    • 3. Phase 3 - Physical Scanning (Audit Week): Utilize Follett Remote devices or VersaScan handhelds to scan shelves systematically from left to right, top to bottom (Follett Community, "Prepare for End of School Year").
    • 4. Phase 4 - Reconciliation & Finalization: Search for unaccounted-for items on hold shelves or mis-shelved sections, generate the Unaccounted For Report, mark remaining missing items as "Lost/Weeded", and close the inventory cycle (Follett Community, "Prepare for End of School Year").

C. Resource Manager Valuation & Financial/Accreditation Audits

Resource Manager tracks capital equipment accountability (averaging over $5,000 per high school student across 1-to-1 devices, textbooks, and lab hardware) and generates audit-ready financial reports (Reports > Resource Reports > Resource Value) (Follett Community, "Take Control of Year-End Asset Management"):

  • Straight-Line Depreciation Calculation: Destiny calculates depreciated fixed asset values using the straight-line equation: Annual Depreciation = (Purchase Price - Salvage Value) / Depreciable Life (Years) (where items without explicit salvage values default to $0) (Follett Software, "Resource Value Report").
  • Audit Compliance Reporting: Generates official Resource Value Reports and fixed asset summaries for district business offices, state education auditors, and accreditation site visits, documenting current capital asset valuations, total net losses, and active device inventories (Follett Software, "How to Achieve Audit-Ready Asset Management").

  • A. The item is restored to Available status
  • B. The record transfers to the Weeding Log, preserving replacement valuation data
  • C. The patron who last checked it out is fined automatically
  • D. The item's call number is reassigned

Q3. What is the correct straight-line depreciation formula Destiny uses for Resource Manager assets?

  • A. Purchase Price x Depreciable Life
  • B. (Purchase Price - Salvage Value) / Depreciable Life (Years)
  • C. Purchase Price / Current Year
  • D. Replacement Cost - Purchase Price

Q4. Which method allows collecting barcode scans wirelessly without being tethered to a Destiny workstation during an inventory?

  • A. Report Builder 2.0
  • B. VersaScan or Follett Remote
  • C. Class Import Converter
  • D. Collections by Destiny
Show answer
Correct answer: B

Q5. Why does Follett recommend rolling, section-by-section inventories during Fall/Winter rather than saving the whole collection for one spring audit?

Show key points
Rolling inventories maintain continuous collection accuracy without requiring a full library closure, spreading the workload and catching discrepancies earlier rather than in one disruptive year-end event.

Q6. During a physical shelf-scan, a librarian finds several damaged, outdated books. Describe the correct workflow connecting this discovery to both inventory and weeding processes.

Show key points
Pull the items, evaluate them against CREW MUSTIE criteria, add them to a temporary Resource List if needed, then batch-delete them under Catalog > Update Copies > Batch Delete with the appropriate weeding reason — this links the physical audit finding directly into the weeding workflow from Module 4.
