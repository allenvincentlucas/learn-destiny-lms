Welcome to Module 5: Daily Circulation & Resource Tracking of the Follett Destiny Library Management System (LMS) training program. This module covers essential front-desk circulation workflows, patron item checkouts/returns, hold processing, fine management, and interlibrary loan (ILL) resource transfers (Follett Community, "Circulation").

Efficient circulation management ensures smooth library operations, reduces lost inventory, and maximizes resource access for the entire school community (Follett School Solutions, "Circulating Materials").

What You'll Learn

  • Perform a standard checkout, check-in, and renewal transaction at the circulation desk.
  • Place a hold, monitor the hold queue, and process an interlibrary loan request.
  • Configure and use offline circulation via Follett Remote during a network outage.
  • Set up a self-checkout kiosk with an appropriate privacy timeout.
  • Apply the correct textbook loan period (class vs. interval) for a given course scenario.

2. Core Circulation Operations & Asset Tracking

A. Desk Operations (Circulation > Check Out / Check In / Renew)

  • Checking Out Materials: Scan patron barcode (or search name), verify patron status and borrowing eligibility, scan item barcode, and confirm loan due dates (Parkhill).
  • Checking In Materials: Scan returned items, inspect item condition, and process instant status updates (Available, On Hold, Reshelve) (Parkhill).
  • Renewals & Special Overrides: Processing item renewals and managing loan period overrides for teachers or extended research projects (Follett Community, "Circulation").

B. Holds, Overdues, Fines & Interlibrary Loans (ILL)

  • Hold Processing: Placing title-level or copy-level holds for patrons, managing active hold queues, and printing hold notice slips (Follett Community, "Circulation").
  • Fine & Lost Material Management: Assessing fines for overdue or damaged items, processing payments, waiving fees, and resolving "Lost" item status upon return (Follett School Solutions, "Conduct an Inventory").
  • Interlibrary Loan (ILL): Requesting and transferring materials between school buildings within the district using the ILL management panel (Ives).
  • Holds and Interlibrary Loans 2.0 (Destiny 24.0): A redesigned, tab-based Holds and ILL page reduces clicks with clearer status indicators and district-level reporting, and can allow an ILL request even when a local copy shows as technically available (Follett Community, "Destiny 24.0 Readiness Kit").

C. Classroom Sets & VersaScan Mobile Tracking

  • Class Set Checkouts: Checking out entire textbook sets, laptop carts, or literature kits to teachers or course rosters in a single step (Follett Software, "What's Coming").
  • Mobile Circulation: Utilizing the Follett Destiny VersaScan or Back Office mobile app for wireless circulation, barcode scanning, and resource transfers across classrooms (Follett Community, "Conducting an Inventory").

Module 5 Supplement: Offline Circulation, Self-Checkout Setup, VersaScan Config & Textbook Policies

A. Offline Circulation Setup & File Uploads

Offline circulation allows librarians to perform checkouts, checkins, and barcode scans during network outages or off-site events (Circulation > Offline Circulation) (Follett School Solutions, "Offline Circulation"):

  • Follett Remote Installation: Downloaded from Admin > Download Tools. Operates as a single-user standalone application on computers or portable devices to collect offline barcode scans into .txt files (e.g. CKOUT.txt or CKIN.txt) (Follett Software, "Perform remote circulation").
  • Uploading Offline Files (Circulation > Offline Circulation): When server connectivity is restored, browse for the transaction .txt file, specify the transaction date and due date overrides, and click Upload (Follett School Solutions, "Offline Circulation"). Job Manager processes files sequentially (oldest files first) and generates an audit Job Summary (Follett School Solutions, "Remote circulation upload").
  • Destiny 24.0 District-Wide Offline Circulation: Upgraded offline capabilities allow district-wide scan collection across multiple devices with automated background synchronization upon reconnecting to the district network (Follett Community, "News").

B. Self-Checkout Station Kiosk Setup & Workflow

Setting up student self-service kiosks frees library staff for instructional support:

  • Administrative Station Setup (Circulation > Self-Checkout / Admin > Site Configuration):
    • Create a dedicated "Self-Checkout" patron record assigned to the Self-Checkout access level (Follett School Solutions, "Set Up a Self-Checkout Station").
    • Configure session timeout limits (e.g., 15–30 seconds) under Admin > Access Levels to auto-reset terminal screens between patrons (Follett School Solutions, "Set Up a Self-Checkout Station").
    • Optional: Enable full-screen browser kiosk mode (F11) and lock down keyboards if touchscreens/scanners are used (Follett School Solutions, "Set Up a Self-Checkout Station").
    • Destiny Discover Kiosk Mode (Destiny 23.5): A built-in Kiosk Mode automatically eliminates session timeouts on shared library devices, reducing the need for the manual browser fullscreen workaround above (Follett Software, "Introducing Destiny 23.5").
  • Student Kiosk Workflow:
    • 1. Student approaches station and scans their student ID barcode.
    • 2. Student scans book barcode(s); titles display instantly on screen (Follett School Solutions, "Set Up a Self-Checkout Station").
    • 3. Student taps Finish (or scans a printed "Reset" command barcode) to log out, or the station automatically resets after inactivity (Follett School Solutions, "Set Up a Self-Checkout Station").

C. VersaScan Mobile App Installation & Setup

The VersaScan application converts mobile hardware into wireless circulation terminals (Peripheral Devices > VersaScan) (Follett School Solutions, "Using the Follett Destiny VersaScan"):

  • App Installation & Pairing: Download the Destiny Back Office App from Google Play or Apple App Store onto VersaScan hardware or camera-equipped smartphones/tablets. Enter the Destiny server URL, port number, and staff login credentials (Follett Software, "Set up the Destiny Back Office App").
  • Circulation & Camera Scanning: Select LIBRARY or RESOURCE circulation mode, tap the camera icon (or use paired Bluetooth laser scanners) to scan patron and item barcodes, and process live checkouts/returns from anywhere in the building (Follett Software, "VersaScan Quick Start Guide").

D. Textbook-Specific Circulation Rules & Loan Periods

Textbook circulation rules operate independently from trade book library policies (Admin > Resource Policies > Loan Periods) (Follett School Solutions, "Configure Resource Policies — Loan Periods"):

  • Term-Based Loan Periods:
    • Class Loan Periods: Assign fixed due dates aligned with academic terms (e.g., "Fall Semester Due Dec 20" or "Full Year Due June 15") (Follett Software, "Set up resource policies").
    • Interval Loan Periods: Defined number of days (e.g., 14 days or 30 days) allowing renewals (Follett Software, "Set up resource policies").
  • Same-Day Checkin Suppression: Destiny automatically suppresses checkouts checked back in on the same day to avoid inflating official circulation statistics (Follett Software, "Textbook Loan Policies").
  • Circulation Limits & Alerts: Staff members receive automatic system alerts if a student exceeds assigned course textbook limits or has outstanding textbook fines from prior terms (Follett School Solutions, "Textbook Loan Policies").

Q5. A teacher wants to check out an entire class set of literature kits to their roster in one transaction. What Destiny feature enables this, and what module dependency does it rely on (from Module 3)?

Q6. Describe the workflow for uploading offline circulation transactions once network connectivity is restored, including what the Job Manager produces.
